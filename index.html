    About this tool

    Two clearly separated layers of trust:

    Verified combos

    Sourced from Commander Spellbook's curated database. Card lists and produced results are shown here; the written steps and prerequisites live on Commander Spellbook, and every result links straight to them.

    Combo hypotheses

    Generated locally by a deterministic rule engine that reads Oracle text. It checks a fixed list of proof obligations per template and shows you exactly which ones it could and could not confirm. A hypothesis is not a rules ruling, and "not found in Commander Spellbook" does not mean nobody has discovered it. Verify anything you intend to play.

    Privacy

    No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no API keys. Card lookups go directly from your browser to Scryfall. Cached data stays in your browser.
